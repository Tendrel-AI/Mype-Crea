# Nutrition table + octagons from a lab report: regulatory reference

Status: **draft v0.1, research only, not reviewed by a competent person.** Complements
`KAN-50-regulatory-rules-spec.md` (does not repeat it; source ids S1-S12 and PA-xx refer to that
file). Date accessed for every URL: 2026-11-11. "Primary" = official text or official publication;
"Secondary" = press, law-firm, academic or mirror, used only to locate or corroborate.

Limits of this research: the paid INACAL texts (NTP 209.652:2017/2025, NTP 209.655:2023, NTP
209.038) were **not read**. Rules that depend on their content are marked UNVERIFIED and listed in
section 7. DS 007-98-SA (DIGESA PDF, FAOLEX copy) could not be text-extracted in this session; its
article text is taken from KAN-50 (S10).

## 2. Q1. Nutrition table: is it mandatory, and which norm governs it

| Rule | Statement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-NUT-01 | **No general legal duty to print a nutrition table was found for packaged foods in Peru.** Declaration is "facultativa" in practice; the one reported exception is trans fat content. The octagons do not replace a table and a missing table does not exempt from octagons. DS 007-98-SA art. 117 lists the minimum label fields (name, ingredients, manufacturer, sanitary registration number, expiry, lot, storage) and, as quoted in KAN-50 S10, contains **no nutrition table**. | S10 (art. 117), N4, N5, S9 Q24. All except S10 secondary. Absence of a duty is a negative finding, PA-N1 |
| R-NUT-02 | **Trigger by claim:** when a packaged food carries a nutrition or health claim, the nutrient declaration is required with: energy, protein, carbohydrate, total sugars, total fat, saturated fat, sodium, plus any nutrient subject to the claim. | N2 (NTP 209.652:2025, reported); same rule in CXG 2 s.3.1.1/3.2.1 (N7); same in the 2017 edition per search summary |
| R-NUT-03 | **Format norm in force:** NTP 209.652:2025 (4th ed., RD 000032-2025-INACAL/DN) replaces NTP 209.652:2017. NTP are voluntary technical standards unless a regulation makes them binding. Binding force of NTP 209.652 on the table format is UNVERIFIED (PA-N2). KAN-50 PA-18 can now be updated: the edition is real and dated 22/12/2025. | N1, N2 |
| R-NUT-04 | **Trans fat:** NTP 209.655:2023: shown on its own line after saturated fat, total of natural and industrial origin, g per 100 g or 100 ml (or per serving or package as stated). If the label has no nutrition information, it goes at the end of the ingredient list. A declared 0 is treated as "no trans fat" for the octagon (rounding). | N6 (secondary). Legal duty to declare trans fat: PA-N3 |
| R-NUT-05 | **Basis (reported for the 2025 NTP):** table or, if the pack is too small, linear format; values per 100 g, per 100 ml, per serving, or per package. Whether per-100 g/ml is mandatory next to per-serving is UNVERIFIED. Engine default: always emit per 100 g or 100 ml (comparison basis, same as R-UNIT-01) and per serving when the user gives a serving. | N2 (secondary); Codex 3.4.2: energy, protein, carbohydrate, fat "per 100 g or per 100 ml"; per serving allowed if "the number of portions contained in the package is stated" |
| R-NUT-06 | **Nutrient order and units (Codex, as fallback):** energy (kJ and kcal), protein, available carbohydrate "of which sugars", fat, saturated fat, (trans fat), sodium (mg), then vitamins/minerals in metric units and/or % NRV. Peruvian order, decimals and units in NTP 209.652 are UNVERIFIED. | N7 s.3.2.1, 3.4.2, 3.4.4, 3.4.6 |
| R-NUT-07 | **Energy factors (Codex):** carbohydrate 4 kcal/g (17 kJ); protein 4 kcal/g (17 kJ); fat 9 kcal/g (37 kJ); alcohol 7 kcal/g (29 kJ); organic acid 3 kcal/g (13 kJ). Fibre factor (Codex 2 kcal/g, 8 kJ) is not in the extract read and is UNVERIFIED. Whether the NTP adopts these: UNVERIFIED. | N7 s.3.3.1 |
| R-NUT-08 | **Vitamins/minerals:** Codex says those present "in amounts less than 5% of the Nutrient Reference Value" per 100 g/ml or per serving "should not be declared". Peruvian %VD reference values and whether the NTP uses Codex NRV-R (vitamin C NRV 100 mg in the Codex extract) are UNVERIFIED. | N7 s.3.2.6, 3.4.4 |
| R-NUT-09 | **Tolerances:** the 2025 NTP reportedly sets acceptable tolerances between declared values and lab results. The numbers are UNVERIFIED. Do not invent them: the engine should present declared = lab value as reported and mark "tolerance check not implemented". | N2 (secondary) |
| R-NUT-10 | **Rounding, "cero", "<" values:** neither Codex CXG 2 (as read) nor any Peruvian source accessible gives rounding or "cero" thresholds. The only Peruvian item is the trans-fat "0" treatment (R-NUT-04, N9: < 0.5 g/serving, contested by MINSA). See PA-17 in KAN-50. | N7, N9 |

## 3. Q2. Octagon parameters: change check (2024-2026)

| Rule | Statement | Source |
|---|---|---|

RM 526-2022 (per 100 kcal model) is a draft; no DS adopting it was found. This is a negative finding from search only, not a SPIJ consolidated-text check (PA-05). | S1, S7, S12, N5, N10 |

## 4. Q3. Liquid vs solid, density, reconstitution

| Rule | Statement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-NUT-14 | Classification follows the unit of the declared net content (ml means liquid, g means solid) and, for products to reconstitute, the ready-to-consume state as the manufacturer declares it. Already R-UNIT-02/03 in KAN-50. **No newer or stronger source found.** | S9 Q3-Q6, Q14 |
| R-NUT-15 | **No official guidance was found on converting per-100 g lab results to per-100 ml.** A density conversion (value per 100 ml = value per 100 g x density in g/ml) is physics, not regulation, and is not mentioned in any Peruvian text found. Recommendation: apply it only when the lab reports density (as here), show the density and the formula on the output, and ask the user to confirm. The same applies to the Codex statement of the basis (per 100 ml for liquids). | PA-N4 |

## 5. Q4. "Azucar total"

| Rule | Statement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-NUT-16 | Reglamento art. 3.4 (as modified): "azucares" are "tambien considerados azucares libres o azucares totales", including naturally present sugars (S1/S2, see R-SCOPE-05). So **Peru's "azucar total" includes naturally occurring sugars**, which matches a lab's "azucares totales". | S2 1a DCM, via KAN-50 |
| R-NUT-17 | Codex (reference): "Sugars means all mono-saccharides and di-saccharides present in food". The lab here sums glucose + sucrose + lactose + fructose and **omits maltose and galactose**. For a soft drink that gap is probably negligible, but the engine must carry the lab's analyte list and flag when the list is incomplete. Whether the Peruvian definition requires all mono+disaccharides explicitly: not verified (art. 3.4 text read only through KAN-50). | N7 s.2; S2 |

## 7. Q6. Additional enforcement points missing in KAN-50

- **Per-serving vs per-100:** for octagons always use the per-100 g/ml basis, never per serving.
- **Sodium vs salt, and "<" values:** a lab "<0,01 mg/100 g" must not be coerced to a number. Store as censored value; for octagon use only if the limit makes the result unambiguous (a "<" value far below a threshold is "below"). Otherwise `needs_review`.
- **Internal consistency checks (deterministic):** carbohydrate = 100 - moisture - protein - fat - ash (as "by difference"; here 100 - 93.73 - 0.02 - 0.01 - 0.13 = 6.11, consistent), sugars <= carbohydrate, energy recomputed from R-NUT-07 within the tolerance of the lab's own method. Energy in kJ: the lab reports kcal only (24.61); factor-based kJ (104.58) and 24.61 x 4.184 (102.97) differ by about 1.5%: pick one rule and show it.
- **Units sanity:** cholesterol "<0,01 mg/100 g" looks like a unit slip in the report (typical limit is g/100 g or mg/100 g with a larger number). Flag, do not use.
- **Vitamin C:** may appear in the table (voluntary) only if >= 5% NRV (Codex); no Peruvian %VD value confirmed.
- **Claims:** any "sin azucar", "bajo en", "light" wording triggers the claim-based nutrient list (R-NUT-02) and, per MINSA, wording other than the MAP's next to octagons is incompatible (R-TXT-06).
- **Rule-set versioning:** record which NTP edition and which MAP/Reglamento version each output used (N1, N8 can change the engine).

## 8. Worked example (lab report, carbonated non-energy drink, 330 ml can)

Inputs per 100 g: solids 6.27 %, protein 0.02 %, fat 0.01 %, ash 0.13 %, sodium 13.89 mg, total sugars 5.50 %, carbohydrate 6.11 % (FAO calc), energy 24.61 kcal, vitamin C 13.15 mg, density 1.0247 g/ml, cholesterol < 0.01 mg. Not reported: saturated fat, trans fat, fibre.

Basis: net content is in ml, so liquid (R-NUT-14). Density factor 1.0247 (R-NUT-15). 330 ml = 338.15 g. Rounded for display only; comparisons use unrounded numbers (PA-17).

| Nutrient | per 100 g (lab) | per 100 ml | per 330 ml |
|---|---|---|---|
| Energy (kcal) | 24.61 | 25.22 | 83.2 |
| Energy (kJ, factor-based) | 104.6 | 107.2 | 353.6 |
| Protein (g) | 0.02 | 0.020 | 0.07 |
| Carbohydrate (g) | 6.11 | 6.26 | 20.7 |
| of which sugars (g) | 5.50 | 5.64 | 18.6 |
| Total fat (g) | 0.01 | 0.010 | 0.03 |
| Saturated fat (g) | not reported | not reported | not reported |
| Trans fat (g) | not reported | not reported | not reported |
| Sodium (mg) | 13.89 | 14.2 | 47.0 |
| Vitamin C (mg) | 13.15 | 13.5 | 44.5 (about 44 % of Codex NRV 100 mg) |

Notes: kcal per 100 g reproduces the lab figure with the Codex factors (0.02x4 + 6.11x4 + 0.01x9 = 24.61). Fibre absent is fine only if the table omits the line or says "not reported", not 0. Saturated and trans fat must not be shown as 0: ask the user or show "no analizado".

Octagons (liquid, phase 2, R-THR-02/04/06, >= semantics per PA-01):

| Nutrient | per 100 ml | Threshold | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sodium | 14.2 mg | >= 100 mg | no |
| Total sugar | 5.64 g | >= 5 g | **ALTO EN AZUCAR** |
| Saturated fat | not reported | >= 3 g | cannot be evaluated; total fat is 0.010 g/100 ml, so mathematically < 3 g. Engine can conclude "no" because sat fat <= total fat |
| Trans fat | not reported | n/a (PA-02) | no automatic octagon |

Result: one octagon, "ALTO EN AZUCAR", legend "EVITAR SU CONSUMO EXCESIVO" (R-TXT-01/03). The decision does not hinge on the density: 5.50 g per 100 g is already >= 5. Margin is 0.64 g per 100 ml (12.7 %); the lab's uncertainty (PA-17) is not stated in the report.

## 9. Corrections to KAN-50 spec (not edited)

1. **PA-18** is now confirmed: NTP 209.652:2025, 4th ed., approved by RD 000032-2025-INACAL/DN (El Peruano 22/12/2025), replaces the 2017 edition. Content still unread.

4. **PA-04:** no adoption of RM 526-2022 found through 11/2026 (negative finding, same caveat as PA-05).
5. **S7 vs S8 (PA-03):** a 10/2026 secondary source gives phase-2 values as current. Still not a consolidated-text check.
